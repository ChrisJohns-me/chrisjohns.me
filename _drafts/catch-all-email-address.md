## Catch-all Email Address

## What is it?

It's a wildcard email address (`*@chrisjohns.me`); you own the domain (right side of `@`), allowing you to set your address (left side of `@`) to **anything you want**.

## Ways to use it (examples)

General Categories
 - `bills@chrisjohns.me`
 - `banking@chrisjohns.me`
 - `travel@chrisjohns.me`
 - `subscriptions@chrisjohns.me`
 - `newsletters@chrisjohns.me`
 - `entertainment@chrisjohns.me`
 - `gaming@chrisjohns.me`
 - `fitness@chrisjohns.me`
 - `home@chrisjohns.me`
 - `work@chrisjohns.me`
 - `online-orders@chrisjohns.me`

Website Domain (See **Benefits** below)
 - `walmart.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `amazon.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `netflix.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `target.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `costco.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `etsy.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `ebay.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `spotify.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `adobe.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `reddit.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `linkedin.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `airbnb.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `uber.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `doordash.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `nytimes.com@chrisjohns.me`

Multi-Account
 - `username_1@chrisjohns.me`
 - `username_2@chrisjohns.me`
 - `username1+walmart.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `gamer_1+blizzard.com@chrisjohns.me`
 - `gamer_56+blizzard.com@chrisjohns.me`

### Benefits
 - Easy spam filtering
 - Identify security breaches - I caught this first-hand from a [Betterment.com](https://betterment.com) breach
 - Identify data being sold
 - Diminish advertisement user-profile sharing (I noticed this on [Amazon.com](https://amazon.com) and TikTok)

### Downsides
 - Confusing to unsuspecting victims
 - Connected services break - rare, but 
 - Third-party or multifaceted systems suck - (what do you do when a healthcare system uses a third-party billing system on another website?)
 - Support on email clients is limited
 
     SENDING emails using this method doesn't always work as well. In-fact, sometimes it can be downright annoying. If you're looking for correspondence using this method, I wish you luck.
     If you send an email from your "bland" email address (eg. `inbox@chrisjohns.me`) and you're inquiring about your account (which has a different email address), some email providers
     require that you set-up an entire email account just so that you can send the email.
