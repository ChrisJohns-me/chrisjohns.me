## NativeScript
Subtitle

In 2018, my team and I were in a tight crunch, with a tight deadline, and understaffed. My business needed/wanted a complete rewrite + redesign of their mobile apps, which at the time was in Cordova. We spent weeks researching how we could quickly rewrite the mobile app with as little work as possible; Xamarin? ReactNative? Flutter? NativeScript? Or just rewrite both apps in their fully native languages, Kotlin for Android and Swift for iOS.

I’ll be brutally honest, I hadn’t done mobile app development before, and my only software engineer co-worker also had very little experience with mobile app development; _no one at the company had done mobile app development before_. The developers that worked on the previous mobile app had left; though, I’m not sure if they were mobile app developers either.

So this was new territory for us.
