## Car Manufacturers

- **Tesla**
	1. Extremely overpriced - similar cars are priced roughly 50% less; resale value is toilet.
	2. Expensive to repair - yes, EVs are more expensive but that's not the issue. It's that Tesla's parts are just inherently more expensive compared to a competitor's.
	3. Build quality - after many years, their build quality has barely improved.
	4. Political association - as long as Musk is CEO, it's just simply a hard pass.
- **GM/Chevy**
	1. Have a history of being out of touch with their consumer base. Tend to be solely profit driven, rather than genuinely working towards their customer's desires. [GM is Dropping CarPlay/AndroidAuto](https://gmauthority.com/blog/2023/12/heres-the-real-reason-that-gm-is-dropping-apple-carplay-android-auto/) Regardless of if they reverse their decision regarding CarPlay/AndroidAuto, it speaks volumes about their desire to throw away their own customer's preferences to increase profits.
	2. Failed on their promises of keeping SuperCruise up to date and expanding. [Bolt wont get expanded SuperCruise Coverage](https://gmauthority.com/blog/2022/11/chevy-bolt-euv-wont-get-expanded-super-cruise-driving-coverage/)
	3. They initially were going to remove multi-factor authenticator apps and solely rely on email or text for account security. Thankfully they reversed this decision as well but this is not a good image. (Even now, it's broken)

## Smart Appliances
*Maybe just **don't** purchase smart appliances.*

- **LG Smart TV** - Investigation revealed that LG smart TVs scan local networks to identify other connected devices, prompting privacy concerns over data collection and unverified claims of standby audio recording. [Gamers Nexus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6IFVTcM28KA)
- **Samsung Refrigerator** - Their ice maker historically have an awful track-record; an external ice maker may be simpler, more reliable, and more cost-effective.

## Smart Home Devices

- **Google** - They make their money from advertising, it's no secret. But the reason why their advertising makes so much money, is that the ads are **targeted** towards you, what you've purchased, driven to, searched, friends, emails, etc. The more devices that they have on or near you, the more data they can collect about you, and the more money they can make off of you. [How Google makes money](https://www.investopedia.com/articles/investing/020515/business-google.asp)
- **Amazon** - Privacy concerns, similar to the concerns about Google's devices. [Amazon Alexa lawsuit](https://journals.law.unc.edu/ncjolt/blogs/amazon-alexa-the-privacy-concerns-when-technology-must-understand-human-needs/)
- **Ring** - An Amazon subsidiary, has been back and forth on how easy they're allowing law enforcement (local and federal); regardless, being a subsidiary of Amazon is an automatic fail.
- **Eufy** - NVRAM password enabled shell login via UART. After a breach and Talos Intelligence CVEs, encryption improved, but AWS thumbnail transfers persist. [sec-consult](https://sec-consult.com/blog/detail/the-eufycam-long-term-observation/)
